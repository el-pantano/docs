## Contract Template for Archibrazo

### Purpose of Archibrazo
Archibrazo is dedicated to fostering community engagement and technical innovation within the Solarpunk movement. As a core partner in the Argentina ALJ/Solarpunk program, Archibrazo will leverage its capabilities in technical implementation and community collaboration to drive the program's objectives forward.

### Role within the Program

1. **Technical Implementation Lead**
   - Archibrazo will serve as the technical implementation lead for the development of prototypes within the Solarpunk program. This involves overseeing the design, development, and deployment of technical solutions that align with the program's goals.
   - Archibrazo will ensure that the technical infrastructure supports the seamless integration and propagation of the publishing network across various nodes.

2. **Collaboration with Prisma**
   - Archibrazo will work closely with Prisma to ensure that the technical solutions developed are in harmony with the overarching Solarpunk vision. This includes aligning technical capabilities with narrative and community engagement strategies.
   - Archibrazo will facilitate the integration of Prisma's collaborative infrastructure into its technical framework, ensuring a cohesive and unified approach to project implementation.

3. **First Node and Infrastructure Propagation**
   - As the first node in the publishing infrastructure, Archibrazo will play a pivotal role in setting up and maintaining the initial technical framework from which the network will expand.
   - Archibrazo will document and share best practices and lessons learned from its implementation process to guide other nodes in the network.

4. **Resource Sharing and Capacity Building**
   - Archibrazo will provide technical training and support to other hubs within the network, fostering a culture of knowledge sharing and capacity building.
   - Archibrazo will contribute its expertise in technical project management and community engagement to enhance the program's overall impact.

## Argentina/Solarpunk Process
Archibrazo will focus on driving technical innovation and community collaboration within the Solarpunk framework. By serving as the technical backbone of the network, Archibrazo will ensure that the prototypes developed are scalable and sustainable, setting a precedent for future projects and initiatives. The goal is to create a resilient and interconnected network that embodies the principles of Solarpunk and facilitates the realization of a sustainable future.
