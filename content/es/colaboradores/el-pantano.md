## Contract Template for El Pantano

### Purpose of El Pantano
El Pantano is dedicated to fostering sustainable living and ecological innovation. As a core partner within the Argentina ALJ/Solarpunk program, El Pantano will leverage its unique position as an ecoretreat center to host transformative events and serve as a living laboratory for regenerative practices.

### Role within the Program

1. **Collaboration with Prisma:** El Pantano will work closely with Prisma to integrate its ecological and community-focused initiatives with the broader Solarpunk vision. This collaboration will focus on creating a sustainable and inclusive environment that supports the program's goals.

2. **Event Hosting and Facilitation:** El Pantano will serve as a regional hub for the Solarpunk festival, providing a venue for workshops, hackathons, and community gatherings. It will facilitate the exchange of ideas and practices that align with the Solarpunk ethos, promoting ecological awareness and community resilience.

3. **Resource Sharing:** El Pantano will offer its facilities, expertise in sustainable practices, and connections with local communities to enhance the program's reach and impact. This includes providing space for prototyping and testing new tools and methodologies developed within the program.

## Argentina/Solarpunk Process
El Pantano will focus on demonstrating practical applications of Solarpunk principles through its operations and hosted events. By acting as a model for sustainable living and community engagement, El Pantano will contribute to the program's aim of realizing a regenerative future. The initiatives and prototypes developed at El Pantano will serve as tangible examples of how Solarpunk ideals can be implemented in real-world settings.
