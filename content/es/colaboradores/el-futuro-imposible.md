## Contract Template for El Futuro Imposible

### Purpose of El Futuro Imposible
El Futuro Imposible is committed to cultural transformation and innovative education. Within this program, EFI will utilize its strengths in narrative development and community engagement to advance the collaborative goals.

> [Contrato firmado](https://drive.google.com/file/d/1-dDWPcq_vouoDxMkiEm5a-JRq-ljAW-r/view?usp=sharing)

### Role within the Program

1. **Collaboration with Prisma:** EFI will engage with Prisma to align its projects with the Solarpunk vision, emphasizing the creation of a cohesive narrative that unites the various initiatives and projects under a common purpose.

2. **Action-Learning Contribution:** EFI will contribute to the action-learning journey by developing and sharing narratives that inspire and guide the community, facilitating workshops, and organizing events that embody the Solarpunk ethos.

3. **Resource Sharing:** EFI will offer its expertise in storytelling, community building, and educational programming to enhance the program's impact.

## Argentina/Solarpunk Process
EFI will concentrate on fostering innovation and cultural evolution within the community by serving as the narrative layer that aligns projects and gives the hub network as a whole its common sense of purpose and identity. The prototypes developed are meant to be examples of making that future realized today.
